# PayGuard — Product Vision

## Vision
PayGuard helps B2B payment operations teams detect, understand, investigate, and resolve payment issues faster by bringing transaction intelligence, risk signals, investigation context, AI assistance, and case management into one operational workflow.

## Mission
Turn fragmented payment-operations data into one workflow:

**Transaction → Risk Signal → Investigation → AI Insight → Case → Resolution → Operational Learning**

## Target Users
- Payment Operations Analyst
- Risk/Fraud Investigator
- Case/Resolution Manager
- Operations Manager

## Problem
Payment operations teams can work across transaction records, risk signals, investigation notes, case systems, and dashboards. PayGuard aims to reduce context switching and manual investigation effort.

## Desired Outcomes
- Faster transaction understanding
- Less manual investigation effort
- More consistent investigation/case handling
- Earlier visibility of suspicious or exceptional transactions
- AI assistance at the point of investigation
- Better operational visibility

## Candidate Success Metrics
- MTTA
- Mean Time to Investigate
- MTTR
- Investigation completion rate
- Case resolution rate
- AI-assisted investigation usage
- Search/filter usage

These metrics require baseline measurement before targets are set.

## Product Principles
1. Evidence before explanation.
2. Human-in-the-loop.
3. Operationally actionable.
4. Explainable risk.
5. Incremental delivery.
6. Security by design.

## Vision Boundary
PayGuard is initially an operations and investigation platform, not a replacement for a payment processor, bank, card network, or core fraud engine.

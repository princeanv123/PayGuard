# PayGuard — Product Sense

## What Product Sense Means Here
Product sense means understanding the customer problem deeply, identifying high-value opportunities, making coherent trade-offs, and translating those insights into a useful experience.

Core question:

> What prevents a payment operations analyst from reaching the right next action quickly and confidently?

## User Jobs
### Analyst
Find transactions, understand what happened, identify attention-worthy items, investigate exceptions, and initiate resolution.

### Investigator
Understand suspicious activity, gather evidence, compare signals/history, document findings, and escalate or resolve.

### Operations Manager
Understand workload, monitor MTTA/MTTR and case outcomes, and identify recurring operational problems.

## Problem Hypotheses
- Analysts may spend time moving between systems to collect context.
- Important transactions may be difficult to find in high-volume data.
- Investigation context may be fragmented.
- Repetitive investigation steps may create operational effort.
- Grounded AI summaries may reduce reading effort.

These are hypotheses to validate, not assumed facts.

## Key Trade-offs
- Automation vs. human control
- Breadth vs. depth
- AI vs. evidence
- Speed vs. completeness
- Integration vs. core workflow

## Product Hypothesis Template
**We believe** [user]  
**has a problem with** [problem]  
**because** [cause].  
**We will know this matters if** [evidence].  
**We can test it by** [method].

## Product-Sense Test
A feature should help an analyst:
- find the right transaction,
- understand what happened,
- determine what to do next,
- reduce unnecessary manual work, or
- improve operational visibility.

If it does none of these, challenge its priority.
